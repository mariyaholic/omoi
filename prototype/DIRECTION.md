# Omoi visual prototype

Audience: someone finding a video they opened, across their personal browser profiles.
Concept: a private viewing ledger, with the intimacy of a bookmarked reading journal.
Three words: nocturnal, indexed, quiet.
References: the indexed browsing of early iTunes libraries; the restrained typography of Penguin Modern Classics reading pages. These are conceptual references, not copied layouts.
Density and mood: compact chronological rows, generous margins around the work area, warm and unhurried.
Forbidden: dashboard statistics tiles, autoplay, invented watched/completion claims, a wall of stock thumbnails, network fonts or production actions disguised as a demo.

Inherit color roles from `design/uika-canonical.css`: ground #0b0913, panel #14101c, platinum #e9e4da, gold #bb9955, lavender #a497d6 and canonical muted/line colors. A local, attributed copy makes the downloadable prototype independent of ignored estate files. Do not change the canon source.

Typography: Georgia for the Omoi wordmark and page headings, deliberately recalling a reading journal; Segoe UI for the working interface; local font stacks only. Base interface 15px, metadata 13px, page title 38px. No font downloads. Layout: 218px navigation, flexible chronological workspace; item detail in a right-side modal sheet. At narrow widths, navigation becomes a compact top bar and metadata wraps beneath titles.

Prototype scope: History with search, platform/date filters, details, save/remove/undo; explained Suggestions; selectable sample Sources and simulated automation; Settings for theme and row density. Populated, empty and partial-failure states are reviewable. All data is fictional; edits are in memory. No Rust commands, browser history access, database connection or scheduler installation.

Review against brief: chronology, visible opening provenance and profile exclusions make this specific to Omoi. The distinctive gesture is the quiet book-like heading and indexed time column, not a hero graphic. Keep borders only for list grouping, controls and panels. Responsive/focus/reduced-motion checks and screenshots are required before delivery.

User-requested Open Design review: read the five locally installed packages and render their component sheets. Preserve Uika Night; derive Paper from Confluence and add a Panel appearance from the Mariyaholic utilities. Keep these optional studies separate from the canon and document sourced versus derived values in REFERENCE_REVIEW.md. Do not copy generic importer-default app kits as an approved product layout.
