# Omoi — A way back

40 seconds · 1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · Original instrumental score

The capture-led cut uses real Omoi screens, close-ups and pixel-art bookends. This copy revision removes the descriptive sentences and extra slogans in favor of brief action words, with no periods in authored on-screen text. The genuine UI remains unchanged. The app is onscreen for 32 of 40 seconds.

## Capture and source

- Captured from the real connected browser UI built from mariyaholic/omoi commit cc530f0af90128c7ff933f80d782d7cfc2a31cf4.
- The film animates camera crops over genuine screenshots. It is not a continuous screen recording and does not depict the native Windows desktop shell.
- Eight fabricated titles and eight fabricated browser openings were imported from two isolated demo profiles. No personal browser history, accounts or playback were used.
- The final film does not reuse the original X video's imagery, audio, layout or product claims.
- The original PC-98 and Keynote media collections were not available in the cloud. The accessible reference guides were reviewed, along with actual official Falcom Brandish screenshots and Apple WWDC25 product footage. This is not a claim that every file in the original collections was viewed.
